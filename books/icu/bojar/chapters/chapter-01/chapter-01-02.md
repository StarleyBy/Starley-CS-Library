# 1.2 Valvular Heart Disease

## Overview

Valvular heart disease encompasses a spectrum of conditions affecting the cardiac valves. The four valves most commonly involved are the aortic, mitral, tricuspid, and pulmonary valves.

### Aortic Stenosis

The most common valvular pathology requiring surgical intervention in adults.

**Etiology:**
- Degenerative calcific (age-related)
- Bicuspid aortic valve
- Rheumatic heart disease

**Severity Grading:**

| Parameter | Mild | Moderate | Severe |
|-----------|------|----------|--------|
| Jet velocity (m/s) | 2.0-2.9 | 3.0-4.0 | >4.0 |
| Mean gradient (mmHg) | <20 | 20-40 | >40 |
| AVA (cm²) | >1.5 | 1.0-1.5 | <1.0 |

### Mitral Regurgitation

Can be classified as:
- **Primary (degenerative)** - leaflet pathology
- **Secondary (functional)** - ventricular remodeling
