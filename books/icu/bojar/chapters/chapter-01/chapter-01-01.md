# 1.1 Coronary Artery Disease

## Pathophysiology

Coronary artery disease (CAD) remains the most common indication for cardiac surgery. The disease process typically involves atherosclerotic plaque formation leading to luminal narrowing and reduced myocardial perfusion.

### Risk Factors
- Age > 65 years
- Male gender
- Family history of CAD
- Diabetes mellitus
- Hypertension
- Hyperlipidemia
- Smoking
- Obesity

### Clinical Presentation

Patients with CAD may present with:
- Stable angina pectoris
- Unstable angina
- Non-ST elevation myocardial infarction (NSTEMI)
- ST elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI)

### Management Strategies

Surgical revascularization via coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG) is indicated for:
- Left main disease
- Three-vessel CAD
- Two-vessel CAD with proximal LAD involvement
- Failed PCI with ongoing symptoms
