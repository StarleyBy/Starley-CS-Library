# 1.3 Aortic Disease

## Acute Aortic Syndromes

Acute aortic disease represents a spectrum of life-threatening conditions requiring prompt diagnosis and management.

### Aortic Dissection

**Classification:**

**Stanford Type A:**
- Involves ascending aorta
- Requires emergent surgical repair
- Mortality increases 1-2% per hour for first 48 hours

**Stanford Type B:**
- Confined to descending aorta
- Medical management unless complicated
- TEVAR for select cases

### Aortic Aneurysm

**Indications for Surgery:**
- Ascending aorta ≥5.5 cm
- Descending aorta ≥6.0 cm
- Growth rate >0.5 cm/year
- Symptomatic aneurysm
- Marfan syndrome ≥5.0 cm

### Intramural Hematoma

- Variant of aortic dissection without intimal tear
- Managed similarly to classic dissection
- Higher risk of progression in Asian populations
