# 6. Cardiovascular Physiology and Hemodynamic Calculations

## Section 1. Right Heart Catheterization (RHC)

### Hemodynamic Profiles and Decision Algorithm for Heart Transplantation vs. LVAD

Invasive hemodynamic assessment via pulmonary artery catheterization represents the gold standard for evaluating advanced heart failure, pulmonary vascular disease, and candidacy for orthotopic heart transplantation (OHT) versus mechanical circulatory support (LVAD/BiVAD).

#### Formulas and Clinical Benchmarks

1. **Transpulmonary Gradient (TPG):**
   $$TPG = mPAP - PCWP$$
   - Normal: $\le 12\text{–}15\text{ mmHg}$.
   - Values $> 15\text{ mmHg}$ indicate combined post- and pre-capillary pulmonary hypertension.

2. **Pulmonary Vascular Resistance (PVR):**
   $$PVR = \frac{mPAP - PCWP}{CO}$$
   - Normal: $< 2\text{–}3\text{ Wood Units (WU)}$.
   - **Heart Transplant Threshold:** PVR must be $< 3.0\text{ WU}$ (or acutely reversible to $< 3.0\text{ WU}$ during vasodilator challenge with milrinone, iNO, or prostacyclin).
   - **Fixed Pulmonary Hypertension:** Fixed PVR $\ge 4.0\text{ WU}$ refractory to acute vasodilators **contraindicates primary heart transplantation** due to lethal acute donor RV afterload mismatch.
   - **Bridging Strategy:** Implantation of an LVAD as bridge to candidacy/transplantation unloads the pulmonary venous circuit, prompting reverse vascular remodeling over 3–6 months.

3. **Right Ventricular Stroke Work Index (RVSWI):**
   $$RVSWI = \frac{SV \times (mPAP - CVP)}{BSA} \times 0.0136$$
   - Normal: $5\text{–}10\text{ g}\cdot\text{m/m}^2$.
   - Preserved RV function (RVSWI $> 5\text{ g}\cdot\text{m/m}^2$, $CVP/PCWP \le 0.60$) permits safe isolated LVAD implantation without the morbidity of a BiVAD.
