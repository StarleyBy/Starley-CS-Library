## Executive Summary</summary><div class="details-content">

### Echocardiographic Views

*   **Transoesophageal echocardiography (TOE):** Provides high-resolution imaging of cardiac structures from the oesophagus; essential for intra-operative assessment of valve function, ventricular function, and detection of complications.
*   **Transthoracic echocardiography (TTE):** Non-invasive imaging from the chest wall; standard for pre-operative and post-operative assessment.

### Normal Echocardiographic Values

| Parameter | Normal Range |
|-----------|--------------|
| LV internal diameter (diastole) | 3.5–5.6 cm |
| LV internal diameter (systole) | 2.0–4.0 cm |
| Posterior wall thickness (diastole) | 0.6–1.2 cm |
| Interventricular septum (diastole) | 0.6–1.2 cm |
| LV ejection fraction | 50–80% |
| Aortic root diameter | 2.0–4.0 cm |
| Left atrial diameter | 2.0–4.0 cm |

### Normal Arterial Blood Gas Values

| Parameter | Normal Range |
|-----------|--------------|
| pH | 7.35–7.45 |
| PaO₂ | 10.3–13.3 kPa (75–100 mm Hg) |
| PaCO₂ | 4.7–6.0 kPa (35–45 mm Hg) |
| Bicarbonate | 22–26 mmol/L |
| Base excess | –2 to +2 mmol/L |
| Lactate | 0.5–2.0 mmol/L |

### Normal Haemodynamic Values

| Parameter | Normal Range |
|-----------|--------------|
| Mean arterial pressure (MAP) | 70–105 mm Hg |
| Central venous pressure (CVP) | 2–6 mm Hg |
| Pulmonary artery systolic pressure (PASP) | 15–30 mm Hg |
| Pulmonary artery wedge pressure (PAWP) | 6–12 mm Hg |
| Cardiac output (CO) | 4–8 L/min |
| Cardiac index (CI) | 2.5–4.0 L/min/m² |
| Systemic vascular resistance (SVR) | 900–1600 dyne/sec/cm⁵ |

### AHA Guidelines for Valvular Disease Severity

**Aortic stenosis severity:**
*   Mild: AVA 1.5–2.5 cm², mean gradient 15–25 mm Hg
*   Moderate: AVA 1.0–1.5 cm², mean gradient 25–40 mm Hg
*   Severe: AVA <1.0 cm², mean gradient >40 mm Hg, peak velocity >4.0 m/s

**Mitral stenosis severity:**
*   Mild: MVA >1.5 cm², mean gradient <5 mm Hg
*   Moderate: MVA 1.0–1.5 cm², mean gradient 5–10 mm Hg
*   Severe: MVA <1.0 cm², mean gradient >10 mm Hg

### EuroSCORE Risk Factors

| Domain | Factors (points) |
|--------|------------------|
| **Patient-related** | Age >60 (1 per 5 years), female (1), COPD (1), extracardiac arteriopathy (2), neurological dysfunction (2), previous cardiac surgery (3), Cr >200 μmol/L (2), active endocarditis (3), critical pre-operative state (3) |
| **Cardiac-related** | Unstable angina (2), moderate LV dysfunction EF 30–50% (1), poor LV dysfunction EF <30% (3), recent MI <90 days (2), pulmonary hypertension PAP >60 mm Hg (2) |
| **Operation-related** | Emergency (2), non-CABG surgery (2), thoracic aorta surgery (3), post-infarct septal rupture (4) |

## Appendix VI: Normal Cardiac Physiological Values</summary><div class="details-content">

| Parameter | Abbreviation | Formula | Normal Range |
|-----------|--------------|--------|--------------|
| Systolic blood pressure | SBP | — | 90–140 mm Hg |
| Diastolic blood pressure | DBP | — | 60–90 mm Hg |
| Mean arterial pressure | MAP | $\frac{(\text{SBP} - \text{DBP})}{3} + \text{DBP}$ | 70–105 mm Hg |
| Left atrial pressure | LAP | — | 6–12 mm Hg |
| Central venous pressure | CVP | — | 2–6 mm Hg |
| Arterial oxygen saturation | SaO₂ | — | 95–100% |
| Mixed venous saturation | SvO₂ | — | 60–80% |
| Pulmonary artery systolic pressure | PASP | — | 15–30 mm Hg |
| Pulmonary artery diastolic pressure | PADP | — | 5–15 mm Hg |
| Mean pulmonary artery pressure | MPAP | $\frac{(\text{PASP} - \text{PADP})}{3} + \text{PADP}$ | 10–20 mm Hg |
| Pulmonary artery wedge pressure | PAWP | — | 6–12 mm Hg |
| Systemic vascular resistance | SVR | $80 \times \frac{(\text{MAP} - \text{CVP})}{\text{CO}}$ | 900–1600 dyne/sec/cm⁵ |
| Systemic vascular resistance index | SVRI | $\frac{\text{SVR}}{\text{BSA}}$ | 1970–2390 dyne/sec/cm⁵/m² |
| Pulmonary vascular resistance | PVR | $80 \times \frac{(\text{MPAP} - \text{PAWP})}{\text{CO}}$ | 155–255 dyne/sec/cm⁵ |
| Pulmonary vascular resistance index | PVRI | $\frac{\text{PVR}}{\text{BSA}}$ | 255–285 dyne/sec/cm⁵/m² |
| Heart rate | HR | — | 60–100 bpm |
| Stroke volume | SV | — | 70–90 mL (1 mL/kg) |
| Cardiac output | CO | $\text{SV} \times \text{HR}$ | 4–8 L/min |
| Cardiac index | CI | $\frac{\text{CO}}{\text{BSA}}$ | 2.5–4.0 L/min/m² |

*Note: To convert resistances to Wood units, divide dyne/sec/cm⁵ by 80.*

## Appendix VII: AHA Guidelines for Quantifying the Severity of Valvular Disease</summary><div class="details-content">

### Aortic Stenosis

| Parameter | Mild | Moderate | Severe |
|-----------|------|----------|--------|
| Aortic valve area (cm²) | 1.5–2.5 | 1.0–1.5 | <1.0 |
| Mean pressure gradient (mm Hg) | 15–25 | 25–40 | >40 |
| Peak velocity (m/s) | <3.0 | 3.0–4.0 | >4.0 |

### Aortic Regurgitation

| Parameter | Mild | Moderate | Severe |
|-----------|------|----------|--------|
| Jet width (% LVOT diameter) | <25% | 25–65% | >65% |
| Vena contracta (cm) | <0.3 | 0.3–0.6 | >0.6 |
| Regurgitant volume (mL) | <30 | 30–60 | >60 |
| Regurgitant fraction (%) | <30 | 30–50 | >50 |
| Effective regurgitant orifice area (cm²) | <0.1 | 0.1–0.3 | >0.3 |

### Mitral Stenosis

| Parameter | Mild | Moderate | Severe |
|-----------|------|----------|--------|
| Mitral valve area (cm²) | >1.5 | 1.0–1.5 | <1.0 |
| Mean pressure gradient (mm Hg) | <5 | 5–10 | >10 |
| Pulmonary artery systolic pressure (mm Hg) | <30 | — | >50 |

### Mitral Regurgitation

| Parameter | Mild | Moderate | Severe |
|-----------|------|----------|--------|
| Jet area (% LA area) | <20% | 20–40% | >40% |
| Vena contracta (cm) | <0.3 | 0.3–0.7 | >0.7 |
| Regurgitant volume (mL) | <30 | 30–60 | >60 |
| Regurgitant fraction (%) | <30 | 30–50 | >50 |
| Effective regurgitant orifice area (cm²) | <0.2 | 0.2–0.4 | >0.4 |

## Appendix VIII: EuroSCORE</summary><div class="details-content">

| Patient-related factors | Score |
|-------------------------|-------|
| Age (per 5 years or part thereof over 60 years) | 1 |
| Gender (female) | 1 |
| Chronic pulmonary disease (long-term bronchodilators or steroids) | 1 |
| Extracardiac arteriopathy (claudication, carotid occlusion >50% stenosis, previous or planned intervention on abdominal aorta, limb arteries or carotids) | 2 |
| Neurological dysfunction (disease severely affecting ambulation or day-to-day functioning) | 2 |
| Previous cardiac surgery (requiring opening of pericardium) | 3 |
| Serum creatinine >200 μmol/L pre-operatively | 2 |
| Active endocarditis (still on antibiotics at time of surgery) | 3 |
| Critical pre-operative state (VT/VF/aborted sudden death, pre-operative cardiac massage, pre-operative ventilation, inotropic support, IABP, or acute renal failure with oliguria <10 mL/hour) | 3 |

| Cardiac-related factors | Score |
|-------------------------|-------|
| Unstable angina (rest angina requiring IV nitrates until anaesthetic room) | 2 |
| Moderate LV dysfunction (EF 30–50%) | 1 |
| Poor LV dysfunction (EF <30%) | 3 |
| Recent myocardial infarct (<90 days) | 2 |
| Pulmonary hypertension (systolic PA pressure >60 mm Hg) | 2 |

| Operation-related factors | Score |
|---------------------------|-------|
| Emergency (carried out before the next working day) | 2 |
| Other than isolated CABG (major cardiac procedure other than or in addition to CABG) | 2 |
| Surgery on thoracic aorta (ascending, arch, or descending aorta) | 3 |
| Post-infarct septal rupture | 4 |
